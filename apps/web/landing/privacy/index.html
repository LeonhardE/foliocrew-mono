Privacy policy

Foliocrew helps you organize investment research and choose which materials to share with a connected agent. This policy describes the information involved in those choices.

Account and device

When you sign in with Google, we use Firebase Authentication to identify your account. We receive an account identifier, email address and, when available, a display name and profile photo. We use these to check workspace access and display your account. If you receive a workspace invitation, our email delivery provider, Resend, receives the destination email address and the invitation message.

Your browser stores sign-in state, local preferences such as your theme, and device information used to unlock your encrypted workspace with a passkey.

Holdings and research materials

Your browser reads the holdings CSV that you select and encrypts the holdings snapshot before uploading it. Foliocrew stores encrypted snapshots, task content and reports. We also keep records of which account owns a resource, its versions, which agent is authorized and whether a run returned a report. These records let us control access and show task status.

Your watchlist and the YouTube channel names, links and notes you choose to save are stored with your account so you can use them across sessions.

Quotes and exchange rates

To value holdings, the market service receives identifiers such as the ticker, exchange and currency. It uses these identifiers to look up prices from Yahoo Finance when a quote is needed. Quantities, cost bases and brokerage account identifiers are excluded from quote requests. The service also uses public reference rates from the Bank of Canada.

When you connect an agent

Linking an account lets a provider such as Muse check and set up its connection. Access to research materials requires a separate authorization for the agent, task and resources you select. An authorized agent can read the content you share for that task. Its provider processes the information it receives under its own privacy policy.

Foliocrew uses connector data to provide, maintain and secure the features you authorize. We do not sell connector data or use it for advertising or model training.

You can revoke the connection in Agent Management to stop future access through that authorization. Revocation cannot recall information the provider already received. For copies held in an agent account, use that provider’s privacy controls.

Hosting and service operations

We use Google Cloud and Firebase to host the service and store account and workspace records. Cloudflare routes traffic to the workspace and connector. To investigate failures and protect the service, our infrastructure may log the requested URL, including its query parameters, the time of the request and the response status.

Retention and deletion

You can remove entries from your watchlist or channel list. You can restore a task from the recycle bin for 30 days or choose permanent deletion sooner. When permanent deletion of a holdings source completes, we remove its stored holdings snapshots and name history. Reports already created for a task remain with that task.

Removing a cloud key archive deletes that archive. It leaves other workspace records in place.

Request permanent deletion

Email Foliocrew’s developer at zxx9808181@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. Tell us whether you want to delete a particular resource or all your account data. We verify that you control the account before acting on your request.

We permanently delete the personal data covered by a verified request from Foliocrew’s databases. This includes retained records for resources you previously removed from view. We also check applicable invitation delivery records with our email provider and support correspondence that we hold. The request covers personal data in logs and backups under our control. Some shared logs and backup copies expire on a retention schedule. If they contain data covered by your request, we keep the request open while those copies remain. We confirm completion once the requested data has been permanently deleted. You can use the same email address for access, correction and other privacy questions.
